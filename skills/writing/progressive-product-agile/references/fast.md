# FaST-Compatible Planning

FaST (Fluid Scaling Technology) is not a roadmap framework. It centers on a
Collective, visible work, self-selection, and fluid teaming around current
work; discovery and delivery happen in the same adaptive system.

Inference for this knowledge base: a FaST-compatible artifact shows direction
and current priority, but neither assigns long-term work to static teams nor
fakes feature-date precision.

## What a FaST-compatible artifact contains

- Strategic intent and desired outcomes for the whole Collective.
- Current focus areas or bets, visible to everyone, prioritized by the product
  owner or product manager role.
- Evidence and confidence per bet.
- Investment boundary and learning milestone per bet.
- Next candidate spaces that may matter, phrased as questions to learn about.

## What it never contains

- "Team A delivers feature X in Q3": teams form around work in each FaST
  cycle, so a team assignment is false precision.
- A feature calendar presented as an outcome roadmap.
- Velocity, story points, or utilization as progress.

## Language

- "Current focus" or "bet", not "Team X's sprint goal".
- "Whoever self-selects onto this work", not named team ownership of a
  future feature.
- "What we must learn first", not "planned for".

For the roadmap layout itself, use `roadmaps.md`. A FaST roadmap is that
layout plus the constraints above.

Checks: collective product direction; no fixed-team feature assignment;
confidence or uncertainty explicit; Now, Next, Later adaptive; Later never
phrased as commitment.
