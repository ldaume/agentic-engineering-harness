# Landing Page

Unlike a website, a landing page serves one narrow audience and one specific
next action. It needs one clear goal, one clear CTA, and message match with
the traffic source or campaign promise.

## Architecture

Message match or hero, problem or desired state, value or outcome, how it
works, proof, relevant details, objection or risk reduction, CTA, optional
FAQ.

Progressive variant: what you get, how it compounds, how it works, evidence,
conditions and context, next conversation.

## Workflow

1. Identify one segment.
2. Identify one entry promise.
3. Identify one conversion action.
4. Make the hero satisfy the entry promise.
5. Put proof next to the claim it supports.
6. Explain the mechanism before piling up feature bullets.
7. Handle only real objections.
8. Repeat the CTA where the reader has enough information to act.
9. Remove navigation and content that serves another intent on a campaign
   page.
10. Test the message, not just the button color.

Length: no universal word target. Use the minimum the decision needs: simple
signup 250 to 600 words, B2B consideration 500 to 1,500, high-risk or
high-cost decision as much as the evidence requires.

Create the page architecture first, then draft each section. Flag a claim that
lacks evidence instead of inventing proof.

## Template

```text
# [Outcome]

[Value + mechanism]

[CTA]

## [The situation / problem]
## [What changes]
## [How it works]
## [Proof]
## [Relevant concerns]
## [Next step]

[CTA]
```

## Golden excerpt

<!-- golden: landing_page de -->
```text
# Von mehr Prozess zu einem besseren Arbeitssystem

Bringe Produkt, Engineering, Delivery und Betrieb um gemeinsame Outcomes,
sichtbaren Flow und schnellere Lernschleifen zusammen.

[Operating Model besprechen]

## Weniger Arbeit koordinieren. Bessere Entscheidungen ermöglichen.

Wenn Produkt, Engineering und Betrieb getrennt optimieren, entstehen
Übergaben, versteckte Abhängigkeiten und Statusarbeit.

Wir gestalten das System, in dem Entscheidungen, Verantwortung, Qualität und
Feedback tatsächlich zusammenkommen.

## Was sich verändert

Teams bekommen klarere Prioritäten, sichtbare Trade-offs, kürzere
Feedback-Loops und ein Arbeitsmodell, das sich mit dem Produkt weiterentwickeln
kann.
```

<!-- golden: landing_page en -->
```text
# Move from more process to a better operating system

Bring product, engineering, delivery, and operations around shared outcomes,
visible flow, and faster learning loops.

[Discuss your operating model]

## Coordinate less work. Enable better decisions.

When product, engineering, and operations optimize separately, handoffs,
hidden dependencies, and status work accumulate.

The better move is to improve the system in which decisions, ownership,
quality, and feedback actually meet.
```

Checks: one primary intent, message match, mechanism, proof or an explicit
proof gap, clear CTA. Reject unsupported testimonials, fabricated customers,
fake metrics, duplicate sections.
