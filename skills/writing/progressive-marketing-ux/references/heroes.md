# Hero Section

Answers at once: what is this, who is it for, what valuable change do I get,
why believe it, what can I do next.

## Architecture

- Eyebrow (optional): category or context, 1 to 6 words.
- H1: valuable change or differentiated promise, 3 to 12 words.
- Subhead: how, for whom, necessary specificity, 12 to 35 words; adds
  information instead of repeating the H1.
- Primary CTA: the next meaningful action, 2 to 5 words.
- Secondary CTA only if genuinely useful.
- Proof cue (optional, only real proof).

## Workflow

1. Extract the actual customer value.
2. Separate capability from consequence.
3. Choose the strongest defensible consequence.
4. Add the mechanism that makes it credible.
5. Remove every unsupported superlative.
6. Make the H1 work without jargon.
7. Make the subhead add information.
8. Write the CTA as the next meaningful action.

When asked for options, write three meaningfully different directions (for
example outcome-led, mechanism-led, audience-led), not three adjective
variants.

## Template

```text
[EYEBROW]

# [Valuable change]

[For whom + how + credible differentiation.]

[Primary CTA]  [Secondary CTA]

[Proof]
```

## Golden example

Input: helps SaaS product and engineering organizations move from fragmented
delivery to a shared product-engineering operating model. Proof: hands-on
product, engineering, delivery, and AI-native experience. CTA: discuss the
current operating model.

<!-- golden: hero de -->
```text
# Produktentwicklung, die als ein System arbeitet

Verbinde Produktstrategie, Engineering, Delivery und Betrieb zu einem
Arbeitsmodell, in dem Teams schneller entscheiden, liefern und aus realer
Nutzung lernen.

[Operating Model besprechen]
```

<!-- golden: hero en -->
```text
# Make product development work as one system

Connect product strategy, engineering, delivery, and operations around an
operating model that helps teams decide, ship, and learn from real use faster.

[Discuss your operating model]
```

Checks: H1 at most 12 words and expresses customer or organizational value;
subhead adds information; every claim supported; CTA names the next step.
