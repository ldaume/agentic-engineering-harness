# Durable Learnings

Agents maintain evidence-backed observations that matter beyond the current
task. Prefer correcting or superseding an existing entry over appending a
duplicate.

Do not store raw chat, temporary status, secrets, or unverified product facts.

## YYYY-MM-DD - Learning

**Scope:** Where the observation applies.

**Observation:** What repeated work or evidence showed.

**Evidence:** Source, command, test, diff, or decision.

**Implication:** Which future behavior or owning artifact changes.

**Confidence:** Low, medium, or high.

**Re-check:** Condition or date that invalidates or revisits the learning.
