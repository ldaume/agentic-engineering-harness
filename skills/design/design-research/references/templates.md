## Interview guide / Interviewleitfaden

```text
Intro (2 min): purpose, consent, recording, no right or wrong answers.
Context (10 min): "Tell me about the last time you ..." / "Erzähl mir vom letzten Mal, als du ..."
Current way (15 min): "Walk me through how you did it." / "Erzähl mir Schritt für Schritt, wie du das gemacht hast."
Pain and workarounds (10 min): "What happened next?" "What did you do instead?"
Wrap-up (3 min): "What should I have asked?"
Rules: ask about past behavior, not future intent; no leading questions; silence is fine.
```

## Usability test script / Testskript

```text
Scenario (no UI words): "You want to share last month's report with your manager."
Task success criterion:
Observe: path taken, hesitations, errors, recovery, time.
Probe after the task: "What did you expect here?"
Accessibility: at least one participant with assistive technology per round.
Never help unless the participant is stuck for the agreed time; record that you helped.
```
