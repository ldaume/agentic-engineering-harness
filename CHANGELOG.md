## Recent releases

- `design-core` 1.6.0 and `design-review` 1.11.0: ten more vocabulary terms name their design loop rule (exact vocabulary rows without a rule id: 20 to 10). The design loop gets `type.heading-ladder`, `layout.proximity`, `type.tabular-figures`, `wcag.non-text-contrast`, `wcag.text-spacing`, `wcag.reflow` (320 px), `motion.reduced` (reduced motion emulated), `perf.layout-shift` (CLS above 0.1), `wcag.focus-visible` and `wcag.focus-not-obscured` (tabbing through the page), measured on 3 clean references, 6 real pages, 4 live product pages, synthetic fixtures and real pages with an injected defect. `wcag.reflow` and `wcag.non-text-contrast` fail the run (both found real defects); the other eight are advisory until a real page confirms them. The chart-line half of non-text contrast is not checked yet. Upgrade impact: a review may now report these ids and exit 1 on the two failing ones; `references/browser-verification.md` has thresholds, exemptions and the fixtures.
- Expert vocabulary for judging Skills: `scripts/check-knowledge.py --vocabulary` checks every Skill's `references/vocabulary.md` (one row per term with definition, registered source, observable marker, and check: `exact`, `jev`, or `judge`). A missing cell, an unknown or missing source, a bad check value, more than 40 terms or 80 lines, and a duplicate term are errors; a Skill with neither a vocabulary, a pointer to one, nor `metadata.vocabulary: vendor|house`, a source accessed over 365 days ago, and an `exact` row without a rule id are warnings. The mode exits 0 unless `--strict` and runs in CI warn-only. Vocabulary structure idea inspired by impeccable (Apache-2.0); no content copied.
- `write-a-skill` 1.8.0: section 4a, Name the Expert Vocabulary: a Skill that judges quality names defects in its field's terms from `references/vocabulary.md`, admits a term only with a registered primary source and an observable marker, lists retired rules under "Not admitted", stays under 40 terms and 80 lines behind one pointer, and vendor or house Skills declare `metadata.vocabulary`; the verify step runs the vocabulary check.
- `design-core` 1.5.0: `references/vocabulary.md`, 40 design terms (typography, layout, interaction, visual accessibility, motion, tokens, data visualization, generic tells), each with a source, a marker, and a check; 15 name their design loop rule id, 20 exact rows are the check backlog. 15 sources registered (Butterick, Every Layout, MDN, NN/g, WCAG 2.2 Understanding documents, Wilke). Not admitted: typographic quotes on rendered pages (house plain punctuation wins), the three-click rule and the fold, APCA as a pass or fail rule. The quality model loads it before judging.

- `design-review` 1.9.0: the design loop measures seven more rules from the DOM (`measuredAudit`). Dom tier, blocking: `tell.glow` (a coloured large soft shadow, a radial-gradient halo or a big `blur()` blob; small indicators and focus rings exempt) and `tell.gray-on-color` (mid-gray text on a saturated background; pale tints exempt; complements the WCAG contrast audit). Dom tier, advisory (status `advisory`, never fails the run): `tell.icon-tile` and `tell.nested-cards`, whose thresholds rest on synthetic fixtures with narrow margins. Audit tier, per screenshot viewport and scheme, blocking: `render.hidden-at-rest` (before any scrolling; collapsed content, skip links, fixed ancestors and scroll containers exempt), `render.broken-image` and `render.text-occluded` (last, because it scrolls and restores; fixed or sticky chrome is not caught). Each blocking rule had no false alarm on 2 real clean pages, 3 clean references, the legit fixtures and 12 live product-UI runs: small samples tuned on themselves. The kicker or eyebrow rule was measured and dropped: the DOM cannot tell a functional chip from a decorative eyebrow. Rule ideas from impeccable (Apache-2.0); own code, own thresholds, nothing copied.
- `design-review` 1.8.1: `type.widow` no longer flags labels. Text inside `td`, `th`, `dt`, `dd`, `caption`, `figcaption`, `label`, `button` and `a` (unless it is a heading itself) and bold text in a box narrower than 30ch of its own font are skipped; a heading is judged at any width. A last word that is a parenthetical or a capitalized abbreviation of up to 3 letters ("a)", "(1)", "Inc.") does not count, so the word before it is judged. A lone last word is reported only when it occurs at a screenshot width (390 or 1280 px); one found only between them in the sweep is dropped, and `evidence.where` lists every band otherwise. On a long clean documentation page the check went from 52 findings to 4 headings.
- `design-review` 1.8.0: two design loop rules no longer give false alarms on clean pages. `layout.phone` tells Jev that the phone shot is the top part of a longer page and ends at an artificial cut; before, text running off the bottom edge failed two clean real pages in 4 of 5 runs (0.67 to 0.80), now they score 0.10 to 0.21 and synthetic overflow, overlap and clipped text still fail (0.64 to 0.95; the weakest, a wide table, goes to the model about half the time). Real overflow stays with the reading audit and tiny text with `type.small-text`, both exact. `diagram.small-text` on inline SVG is decided from the DOM: font size times the SVG scale, measured at 390 and 1280 px against a 12 px floor with half a pixel of rounding; Jev flipped between runs on 15 px labels rendering at 12 to 13 px. Jev still decides it for canvas and images.
- `writing-core` 1.7.0: `quality_gate.py --jev` adds an advisory TypeSafe Jev tier after the deterministic checks: one typed bundle per section (UI instruction, three AI tells, contradiction, universal claim, target as built, provoking content) with the audience, the rest of the page, a rules excerpt and one synthetic yes and no example per question as context; hits are `jev:` warnings with question and probability and never fail the gate. Thresholds 0.7 (0.8 for universal claim) from a labeled pilot that found every labeled defect with 0 to 25 percent false positives on clean text. Questions, rules, examples and thresholds live in `scripts/jev_questions.json`. Gateway or direct key; without a key or an answer the tier says why it skipped.
- `design-review` 1.7.0: the design loop measures type and layout craft from the DOM in the audit tier, per screenshot viewport and scheme: `type.small-text` (under 12 px), `type.scale-near-duplicate` (adjacent sizes under 10 percent apart, each used twice), `layout.edge-near-miss` (text edges 1 to 6 px apart in one landmark) and `type.families` (more than 3), and `type.widow` (a lone last word in a heading or bold text of 16 px or more) inside the width sweep with its bands. Jev gets one visual question, `tell.sparse`, in a call of its own with the whitespace rules, a labeled NO and YES example and shots cut 24 px below the last content, so its context cannot shift the shared questions, whose request stays as in 1.6.0.
